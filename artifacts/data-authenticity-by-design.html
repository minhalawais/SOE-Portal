Recommended four-layer assurance model

Confidence rises as controls move from identity to independent verification

1

Verify identity

MFA/SSO and confirmed ownership of an official SOE account.

2

Control submission

Role-based access, maker-checker workflow and accountable sign-off.

3

Validate evidence

Mandatory documents and cross-checks with SECP, FBR, PPRA and banks where available.

4

Assure independently

Exception review, internal audit and periodic third-party verification.

Bottom line: The platform proves who submitted what and preserves the chain of custody. Independent checks establish whether the underlying facts are true.
