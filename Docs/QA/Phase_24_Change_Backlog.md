### Intake rules

1. Capture in Decision Register first  
2. Classify and prioritize (P0–P3)  
3. Assess impact  
4. Approve / Defer / Reject  
5. Implement only after approval  
6. Revalidate (see Revalidation Records)

Deferred / Future Phase items stay in the Decision Register — not this backlog.
